Questions

Good to know.

What is the minimum order?

55 caps, then in steps of 5 (60, 65, 70 and so on).

Is the price in the tool final?

It is an estimate. Final pricing depends on your artwork, stitch count and lead time, and we confirm it with you before production.

What file types do you accept for logos?

Vector files (SVG, AI, EPS or PDF) work best. PNG is also accepted: use the highest resolution you have, with a transparent background.

How do I send my logos?

The quote tool prepares your request and opens an email to us. Attach your logo files to that email before you send it.

How do I approve my mockup?

Reply to our email with “I approve mockup” and your quote number, or sign the approval block on the invoice and send it back. Nothing goes to production until we have your approval in writing.

What if I want changes?

Tell us which view and which part to change. We send a revised mockup and invoice (Rev B, Rev C) until you are happy with it. Changes after approval can change the price and delivery date.

How much is the deposit and how do I pay?

The deposit is 50% of the order, due after you approve the final mockup. You can pay by credit or debit card, PayPal, Venmo or ATH Móvil. The balance is due before shipping.

How long does production take?

45 to 60 days after we receive the deposit and your design is confirmed.

Can I use different fabrics on different parts?

Yes. Pick the fabric for the crown, the part around the head, and then a different fabric for the top brim and the under brim if you want. Denim comes in 22 colors, and you can use them on other parts of the cap too. Each premium fabric adds its cost once, even if you use it on several parts.

What is included in shipping?

Shipping is $2.25 per cap and includes tariffs. Tax of 11.5% applies to the caps only, not to shipping.

Can I save my designs?

Yes. Designs you save stay in your own browser on the device you used, and you can export them to a file to keep a copy.
